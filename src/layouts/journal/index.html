Backwater Guide

Vaikom, Kerala

Your Day on the Vaikom Backwaters

Introduction

The full-day Vaikom village tour starts with hotel pickup from 8:30–8:45 AM and returns between 4:00–4:30 PM.

Why this experience feels different

A three-hour bamboo boat ride gives you time to experience the village waterways before the traditional craft visits.

“Take time to discover Vaikom’s waterways and village life.”

Living inside the experience

Village activities include coir rope making from coconut fibre, mat weaving, coconut leaf weaving for thatched roofs and a handmade cloth weaving centre visit.

The rhythm of the place

After the craft activities, a traditional Kerala lunch is included before continuing to the smaller canals.

Experiencing beyond the obvious

The 45-minute small canal ride follows lunch and takes you closer to Vaikom’s intimate waterways.

Conclusion

The daily tour costs ₹1,900 per person. Contact Arafath Tours on +91 99474 78328 to confirm your date and pickup details.
